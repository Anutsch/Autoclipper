# Support Autoclipper

[English](SUPPORT.md) · [Deutsch](SUPPORT.de.md) · [Español](SUPPORT.es.md)

Autoclipper is free to use. If it saves you time editing Twitch clips, you can support its development with a voluntary contribution. Your support helps me test new releases, fix bugs, and make Autoclipper easier to install.

[Support Autoclipper on Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/anutsch)

If you need help getting started, see the [setup instructions](README.md#install-from-the-github-download). To report a bug, include your operating system, the Autoclipper version, and the steps to reproduce it.

[Report a problem on GitHub](https://github.com/Anutsch/Autoclipper/issues/new)
